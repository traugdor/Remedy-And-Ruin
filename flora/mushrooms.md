## Notes for design purposes

- This list is a near-ready-made poison/remedy/psychoactive tier system on its own: deadly
  (death cap, funeral bell, fool's conecap), toxic-but-survivable (elfin saddle, earthball,
  jack o'lantern, witch's hat, devil's bolete, sickener), medicinal (reishi, shiitake, lion's
  mane/bearded tooth, chicken of the woods, tinder hoof), psychoactive (liberty cap, gold cap,
  wavy cap, blue meanie, laughing jim), and plain edible/neutral (the rest).
- Low-confidence identifications (deer ear, pink bonnet) should be re-verified before being
  used as the basis for a specific effect — don't let a shaky ID drive a balance decision.
- **Misidentification-risk mechanic: considered and rejected.** The deadly-toxic entries share
  visual "little brown mushroom" ambiguity in real life (funeral bell, fool's conecap), which
  originally suggested a mechanic where picking the wrong lookalike variant could silently swap
  a remedy ingredient for a poison one. Rejected because it doesn't fit this game: hovering any
  item shows its exact name, and the handbook identifies everything precisely — there's no
  foraging ambiguity for the mechanic to hook into the way there is in reality. Not revisiting
  this unless that changes.
